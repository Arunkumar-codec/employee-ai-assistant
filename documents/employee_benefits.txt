EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

1. Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered under a group health insurance plan from
day one of employment, extending to spouse and up to two dependent children.

2. Retirement Contribution
The company matches employee retirement fund contributions up to 8% of base
salary.

3. Wellness Allowance
Employees receive an annual wellness allowance of INR 10,000 that can be used
for gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health services.

4. Parental Leave
New parents are eligible for 26 weeks of paid parental leave (primary
caregiver) or 2 weeks (secondary caregiver).

5. Learning & Development
Employees receive an annual learning budget of INR 20,000 for courses,
certifications, and conference attendance, subject to manager approval.

pet-related benefit — this is used to test the assistant's no-answer /
