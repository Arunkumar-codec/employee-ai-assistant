WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees can work from home up to 2 days a week with manager approval.

2. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance for
employees who are approved for regular work-from-home arrangements.

3. Availability
Employees working from home are expected to be reachable during core hours,
10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, and to attend scheduled meetings on video.

4. Full Remote Requests
Requests for fully remote work (more than 2 days a week) are reviewed on a
case-by-case basis by the employee's manager and HR.

5. Security
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from home.
See the IT & Security Policy for details.
