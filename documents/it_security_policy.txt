IT & SECURITY POLICY

1. Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, changed every 90 days, and must not
be reused across the last 5 passwords.

2. Two-Factor Authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all company email, VPN, and
internal tool accounts.

3. Device Security
Company laptops must have disk encryption and endpoint protection software
enabled at all times. Personal devices used for work email must be enrolled
in the mobile device management (MDM) system.

4. Data Handling
Confidential company data must not be stored on personal cloud storage
accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive or Dropbox).

5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident (phishing email, lost device, unauthorized
access) must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour of discovery.
