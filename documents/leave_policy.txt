LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused annual leave up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR system at least 3 working
days in advance, except for emergency sick leave. Leave is subject to manager approval.

4. Leave Balance
Employees can check their remaining leave balance at any time through the HR portal
or by asking the Employee AI Assistant.

5. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually by HR.
