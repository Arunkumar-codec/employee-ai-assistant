COMPANY FAQ

Q: What are the standard working hours?
A: Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a
one-hour lunch break.

Q: How do I get an ID badge replaced?
A: Contact Facilities via the internal helpdesk portal; replacement badges
are issued within 2 working days.

Q: Who do I contact for payroll questions?
A: Payroll queries should be directed to hr-payroll@company-demo.internal.

Q: Can I expense a client lunch?
A: Yes, client meals are reimbursable under the Travel Policy's expense
guidelines, with receipts required for amounts over INR 500.

Q: How do I report a workplace concern?
A: Workplace concerns can be raised confidentially with HR through the
internal HR portal or by email to hr-confidential@company-demo.internal.
