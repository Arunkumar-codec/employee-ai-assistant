TRAVEL POLICY

1. Booking
All business travel (flights, trains, hotels) must be booked through the
company travel portal at least 5 working days before departure.

2. Class of Travel
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be
approved for flights over 6 hours with manager sign-off.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Meals, local transport, and incidental expenses during business travel are
reimbursed up to a daily cap of INR 2,500, with receipts required for claims
over INR 500.

4. Advance Approval
All international travel requires written approval from the department head
before booking.

5. Travel Insurance
The company provides travel insurance for all approved business trips.
